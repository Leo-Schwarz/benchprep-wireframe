Questions program owners ask.

What makes Benchprep different?

Most platforms serve people an organization already has. Benchprep is built for the learner still earning the credential, and stays with them through every credential after.

How many learners does Benchprep serve?

More than 12 million learners over more than a decade [VERIFY], with customers ranging from about 500 annual learners to more than 1.5 million [VERIFY].

How much does it cost?

Pricing is built around annual learners or enrollments. Content work, integrations, and data access move the number. Flexible models run from 1,000 learners to 1 million.

What content can we bring?

SCORM, xAPI, and PDF, plus content authoring for building new content. Print materials can be converted by our services team.

What does Benchprep integrate with?

Single sign-on, LTI 1.3, ecommerce, video conferencing, interactive labs, webhooks, and a GraphQL API.

What do we need on our side?

Usually a learning or certification lead, an executive sponsor, and someone from IT or product for the integration. The admin tools are built for a small team to run.

How fast can we launch?

It depends on the scope of content and integrations. Some customers have launched in as little as eight weeks [VERIFY].
