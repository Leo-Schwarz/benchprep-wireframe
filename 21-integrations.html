What Benchprep connects to.

Benchprep meets each job one of three ways: an open standard, a named partner, or your own build on the API.

Connection How it connects
Single sign-on (SAML, OIDC) Learners sign in with their existing account, without sharing credentials.
LTI (1.1 and 1.3, certified) Learners reach Benchprep from other learning platforms, and Benchprep launches them into external tools.
Association management systems Learner records and credits stay in sync with your system of record. Personify and iMIS as examples [VERIFY].
Ecommerce Storefront and checkout for learners and organizations. BigCommerce [VERIFY].
Badging Badges issued through your badging partner. Accredible and Credly [VERIFY].
Video conferencing Live sessions inside the course. Zoom Meetings [VERIFY].
Interactive labs On-demand hands-on labs inside the course. Skillable [VERIFY].
Data warehouse Raw learner data in your own tools. Snowflake [VERIFY].
GraphQL API Automate data flows and run bulk actions.
Webhooks Send learner data to other systems in real time: update records, issue badges, trigger marketing.
Content standards (SCORM, xAPI, AICC, cmi5) Import existing courses.
