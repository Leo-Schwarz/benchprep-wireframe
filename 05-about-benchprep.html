ABOUT BENCHPREP

Nobody should fall short of a goal they were capable of reaching.

That belief has shaped Benchprep since 2009. Today we help the organizations that set the standard prepare their learners to meet it.

Request a demo Why Benchprep
[Visual: team at work]

It started with one exam.

In 2009, Ashish Rangnekar was studying for the GMAT while working full time. He and Ujjwal Gupta believed preparation could be interactive, personal, and mobile.

That idea became an iPhone prep app. In 2015, it became a platform organizations use to prepare learners under their own brand.

[Visual: early iPhone prep app]

The first mile decides the rest.

A credential is only as strong as the people who earn it. Readiness is built in the first mile, the weeks of preparation before the exam.

This is where trust, habits, and a relationship with your organization begin. Benchprep is built to do the hardest part well, with you.
