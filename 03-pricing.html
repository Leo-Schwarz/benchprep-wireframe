Questions buyers ask.

We have little or no content yet. Can we still start?

Yes. Our services team builds content with you, from the exam blueprint up, and content authoring lets your team keep building after launch.

Our materials are in print.

Print-to-digital conversion is one of the most common things our services team does.

We already have digital content.

SCORM, xAPI, and PDF import directly. Your team keeps shaping it in content authoring after import.

How many learners do we need?

There is no minimum. Programs we serve run from about 500 to more than 1.5 million annual learners. [VERIFY]

Can we sell direct to learners and through organizations?

Yes, on one architecture: provision seats to an employer or chapter, and sell to an individual through ecommerce, from the same catalog.

We need to run an RFP.

We are glad to respond. We can share a template if you need one, and we answer every question in full, including those about what we do not do.
